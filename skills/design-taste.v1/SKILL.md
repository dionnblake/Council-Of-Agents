# design-taste.v1

Version: 1.0.0

This is a pre-implementation design specification skill, not a screenshot score and not an asset generator.

Evaluate visual language, typography, hierarchy, spacing, composition, density, color discipline, component philosophy, interaction philosophy, platform conventions, originality, coherence, and patterns to avoid.

Make criteria testable. Prefer statements such as:

- do not put every information group inside a rounded card;
- preserve a visibly distinct primary action without relying only on color;
- establish at least three typography hierarchy levels;
- avoid decorative gradients unless they carry product meaning.

Use CANNOT_DETERMINE when the packet does not contain enough visual evidence.
