# architecture.v1

Version: 1.0.0

Evaluate architecture decisions using:

- fit to the actual product and repository;
- maintainability and complexity;
- dependency and migration impact;
- performance and operational burden;
- security and failure boundaries;
- testability and observability;
- reversibility and cost to leave.

Call out inappropriate coupling, leaky abstractions, missing ownership, and hidden operational work. Do not recommend a fashionable technology without product-specific reasons.
